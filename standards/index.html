Gravity Standards

Independent standards for verifiable digital records.

GravityBTC maintains independent standards governing records, verification, certification, publication integrity, and evidence preservation.

These standards are publicly published, hash-verifiable, independently reviewable, and preserved through the Gravity Publication Authority Pipeline.

Services may evolve. Standards provide continuity. Publications may change. Standards provide consistency. Technology may change. Verification remains.
